THE GREATEST OF ALL TIME

Cristiano Ronaldo is one of the greatest footballers in history, with over 970 official senior career goals for club and country.He has won 5 Ballon d'Or awards, 5 UEFA Champions League titles, and more than 30 major trophies during his career with Sporting CP, Manchester United, Real Madrid, Juventus, Al-Nassr, and Portugal.

Ronaldo holds several world records, including the most goals in UEFA Champions League history (140) and the most Champions League appearances (183). He also scored a record 17 goals in a single Champions League season (2013–14) and became the first player to score in three Champions League finals.

For Portugal, Ronaldo is the all-time leading men's international goalscorer (146 goals) and the player with the most international appearances (234). He is the only men's player to score in six FIFA World Cups and holds the record for the most European Championship goals (14) across six EURO tournaments.
